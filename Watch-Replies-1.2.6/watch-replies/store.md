Each new WhatsApp, Messenger or Telegram message gets a page of its own, like a phone notification: the app's sign in
the middle at the top, then who wrote, when, and the message in big letters. Tap the page and a full-screen keyboard
opens: type, press the blue arrow, and the reply goes back through the app the message came from. Swipe right (or press
the crown) to go back to the messages.

- Up to 8 chats, newest first. Swipe up or down, or turn the crown, to move between them.
- The bar above the keys offers your quick replies (set in the phone app) while the text box is empty.
- The first letter of a sentence is a capital by itself. Hold the delete key to clear everything you typed.
- Letters, numbers and the usual punctuation (English letters only for now). A message in another script shows a
  dot where the watch cannot draw the letters.

The phone side is the **Watch Menu** app. Replying works through each chat app's own reply button in its
notification, so a chat can be answered while its notification is still on the phone.

What goes over Bluetooth is the sender's name, the message text and your reply. The watch's app channel can be read by
any device in Bluetooth range.

## Requirements

- The [patched firmware](/firmware) (`fw-wp2.bin` from 2026-11-13 or later) and the [App Loader](/firmware)
  (2026-11-13 or later).
- The Watch Menu app on the phone (the version that comes with Watch Replies), paired with the watch, with
  notification access turned on for it in Android's settings.
- Watch Replies and Watch Music are two separate watch apps. The phone app serves either, or both.
